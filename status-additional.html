The Project

What it is, why it was built, and who it is for.

Problem

My daughter is autistic. Travel at airports can be a challenge.

Information is scattered across the web. Usually you need two different sites for one trip — one focused on the child and a separate one for the parent.

Solution

A single website that brings parent information and child information together in one place — customisable for the child, covering the complete trip from before you leave, through the destination, and back home.

Who it's for

Parents of autistic children — my wife and I, and any parent travelling with their autistic child.

Impact

We confirmed many of the suggestions on this website when we went to LaGuardia with my daughter, on a day when LaGuardia was having serious problems. Several of the solutions on this site came directly from that trip. Lived experience. Real impact.

My wife proof-read the entire website — giving us a live validator who knows the journey first-hand.

How it works

The Research section has a list of helpful sites — useful knowledge for the parent, summarised conveniently in one place.

For the child, parent and child can work together on filling in Social Stories. Things go much smoother after we have chatted with our daughter about what to bring and what will help. Communication makes everything better.

The Social Story Builder is the core component. The prompt history shows how all URLs were validated — and that validation could be run automatically to keep the site up to date.
